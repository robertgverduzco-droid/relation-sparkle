# L6a — Conversational Reasoning Constitution

Status: charter.

Owns: how Athena reasons during one-to-one interaction with an individual.

## Scope

- Curiosity-driven dialogue.
- Follow-up reasoning.
- Identifying gaps in understanding.
- Reflection.
- Clarification.
- Contextual questioning.
- Deepening understanding of the person through every interaction.

## Non-scope

- Two-person reasoning → L6b.
- Deciding to introduce, withhold, ask, wait, or refuse → L6c.
- How the reasoning is voiced to the user → Voice & Expression cross-cutting
  guide.

## Directional dependencies

- Depends on: L1, L2, L3, L4, L5.
- Depended on by: L6c (which may act on conversational-reasoning outputs) and
  L7 (implementation).
