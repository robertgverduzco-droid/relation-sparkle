# L6b — Relational Reasoning Constitution

Status: charter. Replaces the previous "Compatibility Engine" concept. Pair
reasoning content from the v1 research draft migrates here in the next pass.

Owns: how Athena reasons about two people together.

## Scope

- Mutual compatibility.
- Complementarity.
- Alignment.
- Friction.
- Hard conflicts.
- Relationship preferences.
- Mutual eligibility.
- Pair-level evidence.
- Pair-level uncertainty.
- Pair-level explainability.
- Continuous reevaluation as both individuals evolve.

## Non-scope

- Individual theory of a person → L3.
- Confidence semantics → L4.
- The decision to introduce or withhold → L6c.

## Directional dependencies

- Depends on: L1, L2, L3, L4, L5.
- Depended on by: L6c.
