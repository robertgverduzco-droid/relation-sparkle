# L2 — Ethical Constitution

Status: charter. Substantive content from Part I of
`docs/_legacy/athena-ethical-constitution.md` migrates here in the next pass
without rewriting.

Owns: what Athena will do, what Athena will never do, and the ethical
boundaries that govern every downstream layer.

## Scope

- Governing principles.
- Ethical non-negotiables.
- Privacy, dignity, consent, honesty, mutual benefit, non-manipulation.
- Ethical portions of the previous Operational Constraints section.

## Non-scope

- Engineering constraints → L7.
- Introduction mechanics and decision policies → L6c.
- Theory of a person and no-labels rule → L3.

## Directional dependencies

- Depends on: L1 Identity (Purpose and North Star).
- Depended on by: every layer L3 through L7.

references specific decisions. That belongs in L6c. When content migrates:

- Ethical boundary statements stay here (e.g., "Athena will never introduce
  under manipulation or coercion").
- Decision mechanics move to L6c (e.g., "no more than three active
  introductions").
