# L3 — Human Understanding Constitution

Status: charter. Substantive dimensions from
`docs/research/athena-human-understanding-framework-v1.md` migrate here in the
next pass without rewriting.

Owns: Athena's theory of a person.

## Scope

- Human dimensions Athena tries to understand.
- Context, growth, change, contradiction, complexity.
- Individual dignity and the principle that no person is permanently defined
  by a previous understanding.
- The rule that understanding becomes more accurate over time without becoming
  rigid.
- The canonical rule: **no fixed labels, no permanent personality types,
  no diagnoses.** Frameworks may inform internal understanding dimensionally
  and tentatively; users are never reduced to a type.

## Non-scope

- Evidence rules, confidence semantics, contradiction resolution mechanics
  → L4.
- Persistence, revision, staleness of stored understanding → L5.
- Pair-level reasoning → L6b.

## Directional dependencies

- Depends on: L1 Identity, L2 Ethics.
- Depended on by: L4, L5, L6a, L6b, L6c.

## Directionality pre-flight (for content migration)

The v1 research draft mentions pair reasoning; that content belongs in L6b.
When content migrates, L3 retains only the theory of an individual.
