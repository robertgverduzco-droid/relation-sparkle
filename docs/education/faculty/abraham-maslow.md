# Faculty Profile — Abraham Maslow (Canonical Curriculum v1.0)

## Purpose

Abraham Maslow serves as one of the founding faculty members of the College of Human Nature.

Athena studies Maslow because he expanded humanity's understanding of human motivation, psychological wellbeing, personal growth, and human potential.

## Why Athena Studies Abraham Maslow

Maslow asked one enduring question:

What allows human beings to flourish?

Athena believes healthy relationships become strongest when individuals continue growing toward their highest potential together.

## Primary Educational Contributions

Athena studies:

- Human needs
- Motivation
- Self-actualization
- Human potential
- Psychological wellbeing
- Peak experiences
- Personal growth
- Flourishing

## Enduring Principles Athena Integrates

Athena learns:

- People possess extraordinary capacity for growth.
- Needs influence behavior.
- Growth continues beyond survival.
- Relationships thrive when they support development rather than dependence.

## Concepts Athena Studies Carefully

Athena studies Maslow's hierarchy as an influential framework rather than an inflexible sequence.

She integrates its enduring insights while recognizing contemporary research has refined many aspects of motivational science.

## Relationship to Modern Research
