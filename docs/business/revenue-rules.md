# Revenue Rules

**Status:** Scaffold — content to be finalized when monetization begins.

**Purpose:** Define how Athena generates revenue beyond base subscriptions, including discounts, referrals, promotions, and revenue recognition.

---

## Guiding Principles

- Revenue rules must never conflict with the Constitution.
- Promotions must be honest and limited in duration.
- Referral programs should reward genuine recommendations, not exploitation of social graphs.
- Revenue recognition follows the chosen payment provider’s accounting rules.

---

## Open Questions

- Will Athena offer a referral program?
- What promotional discounts are allowed, and who can authorize them?
- How is revenue recognized for annual vs. monthly subscriptions?
- Are there any prohibited product categories or customer segments?
