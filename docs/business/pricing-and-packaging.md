# Pricing & Packaging

**Status:** Packaging superseded by [V1 Membership Architecture](v1-membership-architecture.md);
pricing remains unresolved (see its §17 Founder decisions). No prices, percentages or discounts
are defined anywhere in V1.

**Purpose:** Define how much each tier costs, how it is billed, and how it is packaged into offers.

---

## Guiding Principles

- Pricing should reflect the value of relationship quality, not the volume of introductions.
- Annual plans may offer a discount, but never devalue the product’s core mission.
- Trials and promotional pricing must be transparent and easy to cancel.
- Packaging must respect the [Constitution L2 Ethics](../constitution/L2-ethics.md) — no dark patterns, no surprise billing, no manipulation.
