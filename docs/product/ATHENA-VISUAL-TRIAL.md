# ATHENA VISUAL TRIAL — "Relationship Intelligence Made Visible"

**Status:** Future visual trial — implement only when explicitly requested.  
**Scope:** Visual language only. All existing product architecture, copy, doctrine, runtime behavior, database schema, and member experience must be preserved.

---

## Purpose

Test a more distinctive Athena visual language while preserving the current product architecture and member experience. This is a controlled trial, not a new canonical system. Founder review and explicit approval are required before this becomes the governing visual direction.

---

## Core Feel

- Deep black / midnight-navy dimensional environment
- Calm, intelligent, intimate, quietly elevated
- Technologically advanced without becoming sterile or sci-fi gimmicky
- Romantic warmth without dating-app clichés
- Athena should feel like a perceiving relational field, not a chatbot in a box

---

## Visual Ingredients

- **Dimensional depth and layered space** — information exists in a quiet, layered environment rather than flat cards on a screen.
- **Restrained soft glows** — subtle luminosity used sparingly to indicate presence, focus, and warmth.
- **Subtle lines, nodes, and threads** — visual suggestions of relationships, understanding, memory, and connection. Never literal diagrams or forced network graphs.
- **Structured information panels** — elegant, alive, and precise; never cluttered or dashboard-like.
- **Warm ivory and antique-gold warmth** against the dark field.
- **Soft sage** available as a quiet organic counterpoint, used sparingly.
- **Motion** should be slow, subtle, purposeful, and reduced-motion safe.
- **No swiping / game mechanics / score-like graphics** — no gauges, ranks, compatibility meters, or techno clutter.

---

## Design Formula

1. **Emotional layer** — warmth, intimacy, subtle romance, graceful pacing.
2. **Intelligence layer** — precision, structured understanding, relational connections, layered information.
3. **Athena presence** — a subtle ambient field or presence rather than an avatar.
4. **Human layer** — the member remains the center; technology visually supports understanding rather than dominating it.

---

## Trial Surfaces

The following surfaces are proposed for the trial, in implementation order:

- **Arrival / landing** — first impression of the dimensional environment.
- **Today / welcome-back** — calm re-entry, presence without demand.
- **Athena conversation** — the relational field in its most active form.
- **Living Profile / What Athena Understands** — structured understanding rendered elegantly.
- **Introduction** — human-centered presentation of a potential match.
- **Post-meeting reflection** — quiet, intimate space for returning insight.
- **Waiting / looking state** — patience and presence as visual states.

---

## Trial Rules

- Implement first as a **controlled visual trial**.
- Preserve **all existing copy, doctrine, runtime behavior, and member experience**.
- Do not introduce new interaction patterns, game mechanics, scoring visuals, or swiping gestures.
- Reduced motion must be supported from the start.
- Founder reviews the trial before it can become the governing visual system.
- If approved, this document may be promoted into the canonical design record; until then, it remains an experimental direction.

---

## Relationship to Existing Canon

This trial does not override or amend any existing D-spec, E-layer, or constitutional document. It is a visual exploration to be evaluated against the existing Athena design foundation.
