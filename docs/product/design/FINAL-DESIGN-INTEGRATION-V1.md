## 1. The governing question

When D1–D6 operate simultaneously, Athena must read as one intelligence — not
six aesthetic systems sharing a screen. Voice, light, motion, type, color and
photography are different expressions of the same presence.

## 2. The V1 design objective

The intended progression of first impressions:

1. *This feels unusually considered.*
2. *This is clearly about something deeper than browsing people.*
3. *Athena understands me.*

Maturity communicates before explanation does.

## 3. The core aesthetic

Dark, warm, sophisticated, quiet, intelligent, premium, emotionally safe,
dimensional, slightly futuristic — human where humans appear, abstract where
Athena appears. Expensive without ostentation. The founder reference remains
luxury automotive experience applied to relationship intelligence: precision,
restraint, material quality, purposeful detail, nothing shouting.

## 4. Luxury without exclusion

Luxury derives from proportion, restraint, typography, space, material, motion,
consistency, voice and detail — never from signals of wealth or status. Athena
is sophisticated, not elitist. (D1; Design Foundation.)

## 5–7. Environment, darkness, warm internal light

The V1 environment is a dark dominant exterior world with controlled warm
illumination. Darkness supplies depth, focus, intimacy and category distinction;
warm light supplies humanity, safety, invitation and emotional contrast.

Dark is not flat black: deep charcoal, midnight, near-black mineral tones with
subtle warm undertones (D3; exact anchors remain D-23).

Surfaces may become warmer and slightly lighter as the member moves deeper —
outside → arrival → understanding → human connection. Exact warm anchor remains
D-24; transition behavior remains D-29.

## 8–10. Color restraint, connection color, warm metallic accent

The V1 palette is deliberately narrow. Color communicates state, hierarchy,
connection, warmth and meaning; it does not decorate every surface.

The lavender/plum direction may remain as an exploratory relational accent if it
survives implementation testing, becoming richer and more mature within the
final token system. No neon, candy palette or romantic-pink default.

A restrained antique-gold / champagne / warm-metal family may be explored for
selected emphasis, meaningful transitions, premium detail and Athena identity
moments — rarely, never as ornament. (D3 §"gold is not the luxury"; D-25.)

## 11–12. Material and containers

Material should read deep, soft, refined and tactile without skeuomorphism:
subtle translucency, controlled blur, restrained borders, low-contrast depth,
placed illumination. No glassmorphism spectacle, no endless floating cards.

D-17 remains governing: containers exist for genuine grouping. Broad
cardification is reduced during implementation in favor of spacing, typography,
alignment and material shift.

## 13–15. Typography and composition

Typography carries much of Athena's intelligence and luxury — luxury automotive
precision with editorial intelligence: mature, well-proportioned, highly
legible, contemporary, durable. Larger type may carry more character;
functional and conversational type stays exceptionally readable. Screens are
composed, not filled: negative space, strong hierarchy, controlled measure,
clear anchors, restrained density. (D2; D-04, D-18–D-22.)

## 16–23. Athena's presence and motion

D-01 is absolute: no avatar. V1 presence emerges through focused light, subtle
field organization and near-still motion; the environment becomes slightly more
coherent when Athena is present. Athena is not a generic glowing orb — if
circular geometry emerges in prototyping it must acquire proprietary Athena
behavior (D-02, D-15).

The connection field matures: it communicates individuality, possibility, rarity
of meaningful connection and independence before relationship. Connection events
are uncommon. The field is metaphor, never live matchmaking visualization
(D-03, D-16, D-31).

Default state is near stillness. Listening reads as *I am receiving you* — no
biometric waveform, no emotional-scanning theater. Processing reduces
environmental randomness, consolidates light and increases spatial organization
— no artificial delay, no intelligence theater. Speaking may respond to vocal
cadence without becoming an equalizer. During emotionally meaningful
conversation, motion decreases and the screen becomes quieter (D-30, D-11).

## 24–30. Voice and sonic identity

Production voice direction: warm feminine intelligence with subtly synthetic
purity — lower-middle register, mature, warm, highly articulate, composed,
emotionally responsive, gently authoritative, subtly futuristic. Never
seductive, breathy, flirtatious, maternal, therapist-like, childish, robotic,
corporate, mystical or a celebrity imitation. Athena neither pretends to be
human nor performs being a machine.

She feels metaphorically twenty years ahead: quick to see, deep to understand,
still curious, never superior. Delivery adapts to humor, vulnerability,
excitement, reflection, disagreement, safety and ordinary conversation; identity
stays constant.

V1 prototypes a sonic signature of roughly three tones, one to three seconds,
warm harmonic body with subtle synthetic air — spacious, memorable, restrained.
A signature, not a jingle. Athena is predominantly quiet: no default background
music, rare interface sounds.

Voice, light, motion and sound are one system and are never designed
independently. (D5; D-06, D-12, D-35–D-40.)

## 31–36. Human photography

Humans look human: intentional, polished, authentic, dignified, contemporary —
not over-produced model imagery as the member standard. Five photographs
maximum for V1 (D-07); the six-photo runtime allowance must be reconciled during
implementation.

Introductions center one person: no catalog, grid, swipe stack or comparison
surface (F-31). Progressive revelation runs Athena framing → meaningful human
context → primary photograph → deeper exploration by member choice, naturally
and without artificial waiting (F-33, D-09, D-44).

Once the primary photograph appears, attraction is permitted. Athena does not
argue with attraction; she ensures appearance was never the only information
available. When another human becomes the subject, Athena becomes less visually
dominant — one of the most important integrated design behaviors.

## 37. The Presence Curve

The complete design system follows the established Athena Presence Curve:
arrival (prominent) → understanding (central) → waiting (quietly available) →
introduction (briefly prominent) → revelation (recedes) → human communication
(humans primary) → Relationship Focus (quiet) → established relationship
(minimally present) → return (appropriately present again). Color, light,
motion, typography, sound and layout all support this curve.

## 38–48. Journey behaviors

- **Arrival** is Athena's strongest atmospheric moment: dark field → subtle
  movement → restrained sonic signature → environmental organization → Athena
  presence → brief first words → member action. Immediate and premium; never an
  unskippable cinematic.
- **First use vs. return:** the full arrival belongs to first use; returning
  members receive a shortened acknowledgment.
- **Foundational conversation** is a signature screen prioritizing Athena,
  conversation, voice/text controls, reading and emotional space. No
  chatbot-wrapper aesthetic.
- **Waiting** is an intentionally designed state: *nothing is wrong; Athena
  simply does not have someone worth interrupting your life for yet.* No fake
  search animation, carousel, countdown or scarcity theater.
- **Introduction** is a change in attention, not a reward; the system narrows
  around one human being.
- **Mutual curiosity** is restrained: no MATCH!, confetti, hearts or triumphant
  sound. The register is *something may be worth exploring*.
- **Human communication:** Athena's design presence decreases; the interface
  becomes space between two people.
- **Focus Mode** visually calms the system; matching-oriented environmental
  behavior decreases.
- **Ending** receives dignity: no broken-heart language, failure animation or
  dramatic sound.
- **Return** feels continuous: no "back in the game", no celebratory reset.
- **Successful departure:** the design must permit Athena to disappear
  gracefully; no retention mechanic may override this.

## 49. Payment experience

Commercial surfaces stay visually coherent with Athena while emotionally
separate from her relationship with the member. Athena never personally
pressures a member to pay. Pricing, plan structure, processor, App Store
purchase architecture and placement remain with the separate V1
Business/Payments closure. **No payment implementation is authorized here.**

## 50–53. Accessibility, honesty, privacy

Accessibility is part of the aesthetic, not bolted on: the premium experience
must survive text scaling, reduced motion, keyboard navigation, screen readers,
sound disabled and high readability needs. F-14 and F-16 remain binding.
Reduced-motion Athena remains beautiful through static composition, light,
typography, spatial organization and instantaneous state changes; no meaning
depends on movement.

Design must clearly distinguish Athena processing, network delay, offline,
transcription failure and service failure. Beauty never obscures system truth.

The visual system may not weaken authentication, RLS, photo protection,
counterpart boundaries, deletion, correction/removal, former-partner sealing,
sensitive-inference protections or founder access boundaries.

## 54–58. Implementation philosophy

Coherence outranks novelty: a small number of exceptionally executed behaviors
beats many effects. V1's objective is *complete enough to experience Athena
honestly*.

Existing runtime is input, not authority. Current appearance is not preserved
merely because it exists; canonical architecture governs the redesign.

Implementation proceeds systematically — shared systems before screens:

1. design tokens
2. typography
3. spacing / composition
4. material / surfaces
5. Athena presence
6. motion
7. voice / sonic behavior
8. photography treatment
9. shared components
10. page-level application

Recommended sequence: **Phase 1** foundations (tokens, typography, spacing,
material, accessibility) · **Phase 2** Athena (presence, motion states, voice
prototype, sonic prototype) · **Phase 3** core journey (arrival, onboarding,
foundational conversation, Today, Waiting) · **Phase 4** human connection
(introduction, progressive revelation, photography, mutual curiosity,
messaging) · **Phase 5** relationship states (Focus Mode, ending, return) ·
**Phase 6** administration (profile, Living Profile controls, privacy, account,
subscription/payment only when separately authorized) · **Phase 7**
verification (responsive, accessibility, reduced motion, voice/text parity,
privacy, performance, regression).

## 59. V1 design acceptance test

Evaluate the whole product before declaring V1 design complete:

1. Does Athena look immediately different from a conventional dating app?
2. Does it feel premium without feeling pretentious?
3. Does darkness create depth rather than gloom?
4. Does warm light create humanity?
5. Does typography communicate intelligence?
6. Does Athena feel present without a face?
7. Does her motion have meaning?
8. Does her voice feel warm, wise and subtly artificial?
9. Does sound remain rare enough to matter?
10. Do human beings visually outrank the interface when they appear?
11. Does progressive revelation preserve attraction without reducing people to
    appearance?
12. Does Athena recede as human connection grows?
13. Does Waiting feel intentional?
14. Does Focus Mode feel calmer?
15. Does reduced motion remain premium?
16. Does text-only use remain complete?
17. Are failures operationally honest?
18. Are privacy boundaries intact?
19. Does any screen accidentally resemble swipe-based dating?
20. Does the entire application feel like one Athena?

## 60. The V1 experience in one sentence

> Athena is a quiet, sophisticated intelligence that first creates space to
> understand you, then carefully brings another human being into that space, and
> gradually disappears as the two of you become more important than she is.

## 61. Final integration principles

> Darkness creates depth; warmth creates humanity.
> Luxury comes from restraint.
> Typography carries intelligence.
> Athena exists through presence, not embodiment.
> Near stillness gives motion meaning.
> Voice, light, sound and movement belong to one intelligence.
> Humans are presented with dignity, never as inventory.
> Meaning precedes full appearance.
> Athena becomes quieter as human connection becomes stronger.
> Accessibility is part of beauty.
> Operational truth outranks aesthetic theater.
> The design serves the relationship; the relationship never serves the design.

9. Uncontrolled landing chime in `src/components/landing-background.tsx`
   (D-06, D-35).
10. Hardcoded color drift: literal sky gradient in `landing-background.tsx`,
    hardcoded `theme-color` in `src/routes/__root.tsx` (D-27).
11. No counterpart photography; progressive revelation absent (F-33, D-44).
12. Empty `alt=""` photo thumbnails; no reorder control despite stored
    `position` (D-45).

14. Waiting not yet designed as an intentional state (§41).
