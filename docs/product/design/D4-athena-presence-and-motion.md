# D4 — Athena Presence & Motion

## v1.0 · CANONICAL (2026-08-17)

**Status:** Canonical. Fourth domain specification beneath the
[Design Foundation](./DESIGN-FOUNDATION-V1.md), inheriting from
[D1 — Visual Identity](./D1-visual-identity.md),
[D2 — Typography & Composition](./D2-typography-and-composition.md) and
[D3 — Color, Light & Material](./D3-color-light-and-material.md) (all canonical
v1.0, verified).

**Authority:** Subordinate. D4 sits beneath the Constitution
(`docs/constitution/`), Athena University's Canonical Curriculum, Product
Architecture, Business Architecture, Privacy & Security, Trust & Safety, the
Experience Architecture E1–E8, Experience
[Final Integration](../experience/FINAL-INTEGRATION.md), the Design Foundation,
D1–D3, and all binding Founder Decisions (F-01–F-38, D-01, D-07). It amends
none of them.

**Scope:** Athena's abstract visual presence, ambient movement, listening /
processing / speaking / quiet / recognition states, connection behavior and the
connection field, transition behavior, motion hierarchy, ceremony,
reduced-motion equivalence, and Athena's visual recession as human connection
develops.

**Explicitly out of scope:** D4 finalizes no abstract shape, particle count,
geometry, animation library, easing, duration, physics, connection frequency,
light value, color, haptic pattern, motion token, performance budget or
implementation technology. Those remain open in the
[Design Decision Register](./DESIGN-DECISION-REGISTER.md).

**Governing question.** How should Athena become visually present,
recognizable, responsive and emotionally coherent through abstract form,
light, spatial behavior and motion — without becoming a human avatar, generic
AI orb, decorative animation or attention-demanding character?

---

## 1. Presence without embodiment

Athena feels present without appearing embodied. Presence emerges through
intelligence, responsiveness, light, movement, timing, space, voice and
continuity. Athena never requires a face to feel alive.

**D-01 remains binding.** No synthetic woman, humanoid avatar, goddess figure,
animated face, robot, holographic person or illustrated assistant. The absence
of a face keeps Athena universal, non-sexualized, non-racialized,
non-age-coded and recognizably artificial intelligence.

## 2. Abstract presence

Athena should eventually possess an abstract visual form or behavior
recognizable as *Athena is here* — calm, intelligent, responsive, elegant,
dimensional, slightly unfamiliar, emotionally neutral enough for many
contexts. It must avoid feeling mystical, spiritualized, magical, alien or
like technological spectacle.

**Presence before symbol.** Do not force a logo or icon to represent Athena.
Presence emerges through behavior first; a mark may grow from that language
later (D-15). Athena's identity should exist even with no logo visible.

**No generic AI orb by default.** A circular responsive form may eventually
prove right, but never merely because Siri, waveforms or glowing spheres are
the convention. Any circular or field-like form must develop distinctive
behavior and meaning.

**Athena may organize space.** Her presence may become visible through
surrounding movement calming, points orienting, light focusing, a central or
non-central presence emerging, or the environment responding to her activity.

## 3. The connection field

The slow-moving point field remains a strong directional candidate. Its
philosophy:

- **Independence first.** Individuals exist independently and spend most of
  their visual life unconnected. A person has value independently of
  relationship status; connection is meaningful *because* independence exists.
- **Proximity is not matching.** Elements may approach, cross, align
  temporarily and diverge without producing connection. The distinction
  between proximity and meaningful relationship must stay visible.
- **Connection preserves two.** Connected elements remain visually
  distinguishable. Healthy relationship is *two individuals + relationship*,
  never two people disappearing into one identity.
- **Rarity.** Connection must occur rarely enough to retain meaning. Frequent
  connection communicates interchangeability, abundance, rapid matching and
  social-network activity. Exact frequency unresolved (D-16).
- **Connection event.** May involve restrained changes in light, trajectory,
  spacing, color, rhythm or shared movement. Never explosive lines, hearts,
  fireworks, magnetic snapping or reward animation. Register: *recognition*,
  not *jackpot*.
- **Color.** D3 leaves relational color unresolved; D4 selects none. The
  connection state must be recognizable through multiple signals, not color
  alone.

**The field is not the database.** It stays clearly metaphorical and must never
imply live member locations, actual current users, matchmaking activity,
compatibility calculations, candidate counts or real-time system intelligence.

**Athena vs. the field.** Athena's own presence and the surrounding
environment are related but not necessarily identical; a member should
eventually distinguish Athena herself from the environment around her (D-02).

## 4. Motion hierarchy

- **Level 1 — Ambient.** Barely noticed; creates life and depth. Slow, subtle,
  non-repetitive where possible, low attention, graceful, performant. If the
  ambient animation remains constantly fascinating, it is too dominant.
- **Level 2 — Functional presence.** Listening, processing, speaking,
  transition. Clearly perceptible but restrained.
- **Level 3 — Meaningful event.** Introduction, recognition, mutual curiosity,
  Focus Mode transition, selected ceremony. Rare, and noticeable precisely
  because Levels 1–2 stay restrained.

**Near stillness** is the default state. Subtle life is permitted; the
environment never advertises itself. Timing and easing remain downstream
(D-11, D-30).

## 5. Athena's functional states

- **Listening.** Subtle expansion, spatial attention, increased coherence,
  gentle rhythmic response, reduced environmental randomness — communicating
  *Athena is receiving me*.
- **No listening theater.** Never visualize emotional analysis, voiceprint
  scanning, diagnostic detection, waveform surveillance or biometric
  confidence. Voice is an interaction channel, not an analysis display.
- **Processing.** Elements align, motion narrows, light consolidates, spatial
  noise decreases — the field appears to consider. Never add extra motion or
  delay to seem more intelligent; **no artificial processing delay** is
  inherited. Immediate responses render immediately.
- **System conditions stay distinguishable.** Athena processing must remain
  visually distinct from network delay, offline state, service failure,
  transcription failure and voice failure. One animation cannot represent every
  condition; atmosphere never hides operational truth (X-18/F-18).
- **Speaking.** Presence may respond subtly to cadence, phrase length, emphasis
  and pauses. Avoid a literal equalizer unless testing proves it unusually
  effective. Motion must reinforce her vocal identity — a calm, composed voice
  cannot accompany pulsing, bouncing, flashing or mechanical behavior.
- **Text-only coherence.** Athena stays visually present with voice disabled;
  listening, processing, response and quiet presence remain expressible
  through accessible visual states. Voice is not required for Athena to feel
  alive.
- **Quiet.** A recognizable present-but-inactive state. The screen never looks
  frozen or abandoned; a small amount of atmospheric life may remain.
- **Recognition.** When Athena notices something genuinely meaningful, a
  restrained behavior may occur — tiny change in organization, light settling,
  a slight pause, subtle alignment. Never for ordinary observations; rarity
  creates significance.
- **No insight gamification.** Recognition never becomes a badge, achievement,
  "insight unlocked", streak or reward animation.

**Emotional proportion.** Movement decreases during vulnerability, making room
for thought, disclosure and meaning. Joy may permit slightly more energy,
proportionally — Athena never becomes a celebration app.

## 6. Journey choreography

- **Arrival.** May combine field and presence: stillness → subtle
  environmental life → brief sonic identity → Athena presence → first words.
  Directional only. Arrival must stay fast and skippable in feel — never an
  unskippable cinematic sequence.
- **Returning arrival.** May be shortened; Athena is no longer introducing
  herself and the environment may simply acknowledge return.
- **Foundational conversation.** Athena is relatively central, her presence
  available, motion minimal; reading and listening stay primary.
- **Waiting.** Motion may make waiting feel intentional, the environment
  quietly alive. No search animations, fake scanning, rotating candidate
  imagery, progress percentages or matchmaking theater. A member must never be
  misled into believing a continuous live search is running when it is not.
- **Introduction.** Ambient movement reduces, Athena becomes temporarily more
  present, light changes, focus narrows, the other person begins becoming
  central. Meaningful without spectacle (F-31).
- **Human handoff.** Athena frames → the human emerges → Athena recedes. This
  may become one of her most distinctive motion behaviors.
- **Photograph reveal.** Motion principle only: dignity, clarity, significance
  and natural visual evaluation. No slot-machine reveal, swipe-card entrance,
  excessive blur suspense, countdown or dramatic zoom (F-33, D-09).
- **Mutual curiosity.** A restrained shift — stabilization, a gentle
  relationship between two elements, environmental quiet, small spatial
  change. Never a conventional "MATCH!" animation.
- **After handoff.** Human conversation becomes primary; Athena becomes less
  dominant while remaining available.
- **Relationship Focus Mode.** Matching-oriented ambient behavior decreases,
  relational elements stabilize, Athena quiets, the interface calms. Focus
  Mode feels like making space — never possession, completion, exclusive
  status or permanent commitment. Autonomy is visually preserved.
- **Ending.** No dramatic motion: no broken-connection effect, shattering
  line, falling particles, fading heart or visual failure. Clear, dignified,
  quieter.
- **Return after ending.** Motion may communicate renewed openness without
  pretending the past disappeared. No celebratory reset, no "back in the game".
- **Successful departure.** Motion becomes extremely restrained; Athena may
  simply become quiet. The most sophisticated ceremony involves less, not more.

## 7. The Athena Presence Curve (visual expression)

| Stage | Visual presence |
|---|---|
| Arrival | High |
| Understanding | High but familiar |
| Waiting | Low to moderate |
| Introduction | Temporarily increased |
| Mutual curiosity | Beginning to yield |
| Human connection | Lower |
| Relationship Focus | Low |
| Established relationship | Minimal, available |
| Return | Rises appropriately again |

Motion communicates Athena's changing role. She never needs to announce "I am
stepping back now" — the interface creates more space.

## 8. Everyday motion

- **Page transitions** are quick, smooth and restrained. No cinematic
  transitions for ordinary navigation.
- **Microinteractions** (press, save, toggle, correction, mode change, form
  success) are fast, subtle and functional. They never borrow the ceremony
  language of meaningful relationship events.
- **Haptics** remain open (D-13). They may eventually complement functional
  confirmations, significant transitions and safety warnings, and must never
  become reward conditioning. None selected here.
- **Sonic/motion synchronization.** Sound and motion are developed together; a
  short sonic identity may correspond with Athena appearance, light and
  spatial response. Do not compose sound independently of motion (D-06).
- **Typography** mostly stays stable. Text may occasionally enter with
  restrained motion; no slow typewriter effects, bouncing words,
  over-animated typography or cinematic title sequences during normal use.
- **Streaming text.** Current streaming is operationally honest; preserve that
  honesty. Presentation may be refined, never by manufacturing typing delay.
- **Scroll and parallax.** Content scrolls naturally; no scroll hijacking. Use
  parallax sparingly or not at all — it creates sensory fatigue, complicates
  accessibility and dates quickly.

## 9. Reduced motion is a first-class state

F-16 remains binding. Under `prefers-reduced-motion`: continuous ambient
animation stops or substantially reduces, state changes stay perceptible,
hierarchy stays intact, no essential meaning disappears, the connection-field
metaphor may become static, and transitions simplify.

The reduced-motion experience must never look like effects broke. Static
equivalents remain composed, intentional and elegant.

**Motion accessibility.** No flashing, rapid zoom, large repeated scaling,
vestibular triggers or unnecessary looping. Accessibility outranks animation
ambition (F-14).

## 10. Performance and honesty

- **Performance.** Motion stays smooth on realistically supported devices; a
  beautiful animation that stutters is not premium. A performance budget is
  required downstream (D-33).
- **Resource respect.** Continuous ambient behavior is evaluated for CPU, GPU,
  battery and thermal cost. Athena's atmosphere must not create unnecessary
  device cost.
- **Offline / failure.** Visual presence must never pretend normal operation
  during offline, timeout or service failure. Aesthetic state and system state
  stay separate.
- **Privacy.** Motion must never expose private information — no external
  animations or previews communicating relationship state, incoming sensitive
  content or introduction identity the member has not chosen to reveal.
- **Visual honesty.** Do not animate "searching" when no search occurs,
  "analyzing voice" when no analysis occurs, "finding connection" when the
  field is decorative, or "loading" after content is available. Metaphor may
  exist; deception may not.

## 11. A proprietary, durable motion language

Athena should develop a small set of recognizable behaviors — arrival,
listening, processing, recognition, connection, recession. A few coherent
behaviors are stronger than dozens of animations. Movement stays organic,
precise, restrained and durable, avoiding trend-dependent styles.

**Three-second recognition** is the long-term objective: a few seconds of
Athena's motion, without logo or text, should plausibly identify the
experience — through coherence, not forced novelty.

## 12. Tests

**Motion restraint test.** If we remove this motion, what meaning disappears?
If none, seriously consider removal.

**Athena presence test.** Does it feel intelligent? Calm? Does it avoid
impersonating humanity? Avoid generic AI conventions? Support listening,
processing, speaking and quiet? Work without sound? Survive reduced motion?
Feel gender-neutral? Coexist with human photography without competing? Recede
gracefully? Remain recognizable over time? Feel slightly futuristic without
sci-fi theater?

**Connection field test.** Is connection rare enough to matter? Do independent
elements remain valuable? Is proximity distinct from connection? Is the field
clearly atmospheric rather than functional data? Does it avoid abundance
signaling? Preserve reduced-motion equivalence? Perform well? Look proprietary
rather than like a stock particle effect? Support both arrival and later
Athena states? Stay interesting without distracting?

## 13. Governing principles

> Athena feels present without becoming embodied.
> Presence emerges through behavior before symbol.
> Near stillness gives movement meaning.
> Connection is rare enough to matter.
> Independence exists before connection.
> Proximity is not matching.
> Connection preserves two individuals.
> Athena's processing state communicates work without performing intelligence.
> Motion must distinguish real operational states.
> Athena frames the introduction; the human eventually becomes the visual event.
> Athena's visual presence follows the Presence Curve and recedes as human connection grows.
> Reduced motion remains a complete Athena experience.
> Metaphor may be atmospheric; it must never become deceptive.
> A few proprietary motion behaviors are stronger than constant animation.
> Sometimes Athena's most sophisticated movement is becoming still.

### Runtime evidence and newly discovered divergences (not repaired)

- **Merging pairs contradict §3 "connection preserves two".**
  `src/components/landing-background.tsx` pairs two points, draws them
  together, and at `dist < 3` sets `merged` — two individuals literally fuse
  into one object. This is the fusion model D4 rejects. Recorded as D-31.
- **Connection abundance.** Pairing recurs frequently enough to read as
  abundance rather than rarity (D-16, §3).
- **1200ms global route entrance.** `@utility fade-in-slow` applies a
  1200ms entrance broadly; D4 §8 asks for quick, restrained routine
  transitions. Evidence only — evaluate downstream against "calm ≠ latency"
  (D-32).
- **Generic pulse states.** `animate-pulse` is used for the recording state
  and the thinking indicator in `src/routes/_authenticated/athena.tsx`, and in
  the skeleton component — generic, not Athena-native, and does not
  distinguish processing from network or service conditions (§5, F-18).
- Carried forward from D1–D3: six-photo uploader (D-07), broad cardification,
  hardcoded landing sky gradient and `theme-color` (D-27), theme-shaped
  light/dark pair (D-28), three sub-44px links.
